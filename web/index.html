Overview

The Problem

Patients with rare autoimmune diseases — Systemic Lupus Erythematosus, Sjogren's Syndrome, Undifferentiated Connective Tissue Disease — often wait years before receiving a diagnosis. Existing computational models rely on Electronic Health Records, which suffer from a fundamental left-censoring bias: data collection begins only after the patient has already reached secondary care.

This project bypasses that limitation entirely by listening to patients where they first speak — public health communities on Reddit — and modelling the full diagnostic odyssey from the patient's own evolving narrative.

Research Questions
RQ 1 — Feasibility

Can longitudinal language trajectories and posting-interval dynamics from public health forums reliably predict diagnostic delays exceeding one year, before any clinical record exists?

RQ 2 — Biomarkers

Which narrative signals — physician dismissal, multi-organ symptom co-mention, frustration slope, or temporal burstiness — carry the strongest predictive weight for extended delays?

RQ 3 — Explainability

Can sequence architectures not only classify delay risk but also pinpoint the exact post in a patient's timeline that most significantly shifts the predicted trajectory?

System Pipeline

Seven-stage architecture from raw social text to an interpretable, privacy-safe clinical risk score.

01Data IngestionPRAW API + Arctic Shift dumps from 6 subreddits

03Weak SupervisionRegex extraction of self-reported delay durations
04Timeline WindowPrune diagnosis post; build pre-diagnostic sequence
05Feature EngineeringLexicons + S-BERT embeddings + temporal + sentiment
06Sequence ModelBiLSTM + Bahdanau attention or Time2Vec Transformer

Model Architectures
Proposed · Best Performance
Longitudinal BiLSTM + Bahdanau Attention
  • 2-layer Bidirectional LSTM, hidden dim H = 64 (concatenated: 128)
  • Post-level additive attention weights αt
  • Context vector c = Σ αt · ht
  • MLP head · GELU · Dropout 0.30 · AdamW · lr 1e-3
  • AUROC 0.978 · F1 0.945 · Accuracy 94.2%
Proposed · Time-Aware
Temporal Transformer (Time2Vec)
  • Time2Vec continuous interval encoding, d_time = 16
  • Transformer Encoder: d_model = 64, 4 attention heads, 2 layers
  • Global average pooling + classification head
  • Models non-linear and periodic temporal interval patterns
  • AUROC 0.966 · F1 0.928 · Accuracy 92.7%

Ethics & Privacy

AoIR Compliance

  • Salted SHA-256 Pseudonymization — usernames hashed with a private cryptographic salt, yielding PATIENT_[HEX12] identifiers. Salt is never co-located with data.
  • Multi-Tier PII Scrubbing — regex redacts physician names, institutions, emails, phone numbers, URLs, and @mentions before tokenization.
  • Anti-Leakage Timeline Pruning — the diagnosis-confirmation post is automatically removed from all model inputs.
  • Paraphrased Case Studies — no verbatim patient quotes appear in any published output. All illustrative text is synthetic or paraphrased.
  • Data Minimization — only post text, timestamp, and subreddit are collected. No engagement metrics or account history are stored.
